## Decisions & trade-offs
- **All four decisions taken as recommended:** the three checks, a citext name, no cascades, and the "current batches" index.
- **Dates as strings (`mode: 'string'`).** A `date` has no time zone; reading it as a JS `Date` would shift it by the server's offset. The domain (6.2) can parse it.
- **The schema imports `users` from identity** for the foreign keys. That's the only cross-context link, and it's in infrastructure only.
- **Rules the database can't express** stay for 6.2: a member must hold the matching global role, and an archived batch is read-only.

## Deviations from plan
- None in substance. I added "any number of current mentors" as a check that the one-PM index really is limited to PMs, and it's what caught the "without `role`" mutation.
- I first saved this task's PLAN.md under a mistyped folder (`06-batches/`). It was moved into `06-batches-cohort/`, and the stray folder removed, before you approved.
